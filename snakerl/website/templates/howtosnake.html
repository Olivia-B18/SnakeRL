How To Play Snake


The Goals


Grow the snake. Make your snake (reperesented by a series of green squares) longer by eating apples (represented by a red square).

Don't crash. Be careful to not let the snake crash into walls or into itself!

Differences in Snake for Human Players vs AI Agents in SnakeRL


Best scores. When you play Snake, the best score is about 9 points while a good score is about 5. When the RL agent plays Snake, the best score known so far is about 57 while a good score is about 20.

Game speed. When you play Snake, the game will speed up each round to make things more challenging. When the RL agent learns how to play, its games won't be sped up.
