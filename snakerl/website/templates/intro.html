Introduction

What is Reinforcement Learning?


If you reward a chick when it does a backflip, it will do more backflips. Similarly, if you punish the chick when it misbehaves, it won't misbehave as much.

In the same way, you can teach an AI agent how to accomplish its task by having it learn through trial and error. This is the core concept of reinforcement learning (RL).

Usually, algorithms are told exactly what to do for every scenario, but this step-by-step direction is not always feasible. In the case of the chick, it's difficult to tell it exactly how hard to push off the ground, especially when you must consider how solid the ground is or how windy it may be or what angle the wings should be at for counterbalancing or any number of other factors.

Realistically, we are not teaching chicks backflips, but people do teach robots how to walk, train cars to drive autonomously, and help satellites orbit despite loss of a sensor. Some tasks can become automated for convenience while others, like the satellite, are impossible for a human to do.

In SnakeRL, you will learn the basics of RL by teaching an AI agent how to play the classic game Snake. But first... try playing it yourself!
